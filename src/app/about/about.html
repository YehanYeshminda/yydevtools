About YYDevTools

YYDevTools is a free collection of fast, focused utilities for developers and anyone who works with files — JSON, JWTs, hashing, regular expressions, colour, plus a full set of PDF tools, all in one place.

Open a tool and start. There is no account to create, nothing to install, and most tools do their work entirely in your browser, so your data stays with you.

Why YYDevTools exists

Most small development tasks — pretty-printing a JSON payload, decoding a token to see what is inside it, checking a file's checksum, shrinking a PDF before you email it — don't need a desktop app or a sign-up wall. They need a page that loads instantly, does one thing well, and forgets about you the moment you close the tab.

YYDevTools is that page, repeated for the couple of dozen jobs that come up most often. Each tool is deliberately small and single-purpose, with a clean interface, sensible defaults and clear error messages, so you can get in, get the answer and get back to what you were actually doing.

How your data is handled

Privacy is built into how the tools are made, not bolted on afterwards. Formatting, decoding, hashing, diffing, colour conversion, QR generation and even most image and PDF work happen with code that runs in your browser — the file or text you paste in is processed on your own machine and is never sent to a server.

A few heavier PDF operations are the exception: converting a PDF to an editable Office document, compressing one, and running text recognition on long or non-English scans use a small hosted service that processes the file and returns the result without keeping it. Which tools stay local and which use that service is spelled out on the Privacy page.

The ideas behind the tools

If you want to understand a concept rather than just run a tool, the guides are plain-English explainers on the ideas the tools touch — what a JWT really is and why decoding isn't verifying, how Base64 works, the difference between hashing, encryption and encoding, how cron schedules are read, and more. There's also a tech news feed to keep an eye on what's happening across the wider industry.

How it's built

YYDevTools is a single-page app built with Angular and prerendered to static HTML, then served from Cloudflare's edge so pages arrive quickly wherever you are. The in-browser tools lean on well-tested open-source libraries and WebAssembly for the heavy lifting, and the few server-side PDF operations are handled by open-source engines such as Ghostscript, OCRmyPDF and LibreOffice.

The whole project is open source. The code lives on GitHub, where you can read how any tool works, report a bug, or suggest one that's missing — or reach out through the contact page.

Letters A to Z and digits change; punctuation, emoji and other alphabets stay as they are. Pasting styled text shows the plain version below.

These are not fonts. Each styled letter is a different Unicode character, often a mathematical symbol, so screen readers read them badly or not at all: some spell out "mathematical bold capital H" letter by letter, some skip them. Search engines may not index them as words either. Keep names, key information and hashtags in plain text, and use styling for a word or two. Why this happens
